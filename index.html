FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

Do you charge a monthly fee?

No. The build is a one-time fee. Hosting and upkeep are billed yearly ($390 for a new site, $490 for a redesign).

What is included in hosting & upkeep?

Keeping the site online, basic security/updates for the stack we use, and small text or contact changes when you ask.

Do I need to buy a domain?

If you already have a domain, we can use it. If you need a new one — including a paid WordPress domain — that cost is billed separately.

Can I see examples before deciding?

Yes. The sample concepts on this page show the style and structure. We can also prepare a draft demo for your business before you commit.

Who is this for?

Small local service businesses in the US, UK, and Australia — cleaning, plumbing, salons, auto shops, and similar trades that need a clear website customers can trust.
